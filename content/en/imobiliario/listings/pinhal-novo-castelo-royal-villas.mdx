## Property Description

Contemporary villa located in the exclusive Castelo Royal Villas condominium in Pinhal Novo. This property stands out for its high-quality finishes, modern design and functional layout.

## Main Features

The villa is spread across two floors with spacious and bright interiors. The ground floor includes an open-plan living and dining room with direct access to the garden and pool, a fully equipped kitchen with premium brand appliances, and one suite.

The first floor includes three suites, all with built-in wardrobes and private balconies. The main suite includes a walk-in closet and a bathroom with a hydromassage tub.

## Outdoor Areas

Private 500 m2 garden with a heated pool, wooden deck and barbecue area. Closed garage for two vehicles with automatic gate.

## Location

Located in Pinhal Novo, with close access to the A2 and A13 motorways, allowing easy travel to Lisbon and Setubal. The condominium includes 24-hour security, green areas and a children's playground.

## Technical Specifications

- Solar panels for water heating
- Central climate control system
- Double glazing with thermal break
- Integrated smart home system
- A+ energy certification
