## Description

Historic property built in the 19th century and fully restored while preserving original decorative elements: ornate stucco, noble wood ceilings, signature tiles and period ironwork. It is spread across three floors with a noble hall, library, multiple reception rooms, six suites and service quarters.

## Gardens and surroundings

The manor house sits on landscaped romantic gardens with an ornamental lake and several sub-areas inspired by 19th-century design. The surroundings include fully private comfort areas.

## Heritage context

Located in the town of Sintra, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Immediate proximity to major landmarks such as the National Palace, Pena Palace and Quinta da Regaleira, as well as the region's cultural and gastronomic offerings.
