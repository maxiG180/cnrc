## Equipment and Technology

The special forced-entry vehicle is fully equipped with cutting-edge technology and tools, designed to ensure total autonomy in any operation. This operational resource is a decisive asset in procedures that require fast, safe and controlled access to enclosed spaces.

The equipment includes specialised tools for different types of locks, reinforced doors and security systems, enabling effective intervention in complex or technically demanding scenarios.

## Operational Capabilities

With this vehicle, we can perform forced entries with surgical precision, minimising collateral damage and preserving the integrity of assets inside. Access to critical areas is significantly accelerated, increasing procedure efficiency and reducing intervention time.

Operations always take place in complete safety, thanks to specialised equipment and rigorous protocols implemented by the team. The vehicle's versatility allows action in any context, from densely populated urban environments to remote, hard-to-reach areas.

## Effectiveness and Safety

This resource sets us apart by enabling fast, controlled and effective action, ensuring every operation runs with maximum rigour and safety. The reinforcement of the team's technical capacity guarantees a professional and highly prepared response, fundamental to the success of the most demanding procedures.
