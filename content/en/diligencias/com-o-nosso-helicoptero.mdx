## A distinctive pillar of our capability

CNRC's helicopter is one of the most distinctive pillars of our operational capacity. We are the **only credit recovery firm in Portugal and the Schengen area** with an in-house aerial resource.

## Operational applications

### Vehicle location
Agile identification of vehicles in car parks, warehouses or wide-open terrain, with direct coordination with ground teams for immediate seizure.

### Machinery and equipment location
On construction sites, yards or industrial zones, the helicopter enables aerial reconnaissance without premature intervention on the ground.

### Animal location
Rapid interventions to locate animals on scattered farms, where there is a risk of dispersal or concealment.

### Operation monitoring
Aerial follow-up of the fleet during transport of seized assets and real-time coordination.
