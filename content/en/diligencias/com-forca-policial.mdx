## Coordination protocol

Every procedure with police force follows a strict protocol, beginning with a formal request to the competent authority and accompanied by all relevant procedural documentation. The police presence is requested to ensure the legality of the intervention and the safety of the teams on the ground.

## Operational coordination

On the eve of the operation, a joint briefing is held with the assigned police officers. The briefing reviews each party's role, the profile of the location, the contingency plan and the real-time communication rules.

## Transparency and legality

Every step is documented. The final report includes written, photographic and, where applicable, video records, ready to be filed with the court and to confirm the full legal compliance of the procedure.
