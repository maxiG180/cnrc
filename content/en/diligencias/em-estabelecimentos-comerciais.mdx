## Logistical preparation

Commercial premises demand careful planning: inventory, goods handling, equipment disassembly and, in many cases, coordination with police forces. Each step is detailed in the operational briefing held the night before.

## Preserving economic value

Our goal is to maximise recovery value. Seized goods are handled with care, properly packed and transported to secure facilities, where they await the next stage of the enforcement process.

## Procedural reporting

Each procedure closes with a detailed report, inventory and photographic support, ensuring full traceability and easy integration into the judicial case file.
