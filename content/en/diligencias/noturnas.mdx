## Reasons to act at night

Some operations require nocturnal intervention: vehicles or machinery only present at the location overnight, premises that operate exclusively at night, or scenarios where daytime intervention would jeopardise the effectiveness of the procedure.

## Specific preparation

Operating at night brings its own challenges: reduced visibility, more critical police presence, the need for adequate lighting and heightened risk management. The previous evening's operational briefing covers all of these, with contingency plans tailored to the time of day.

## Same rigour, same transparency

Whatever the time, every procedure is fully documented. The final report includes a photographic record and, whenever possible, video, ensuring complete traceability and procedural compliance.
