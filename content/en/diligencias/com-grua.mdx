## When scale demands in-house resources

Seizing large-scale assets requires specific equipment and meticulous coordination. Owning our own fleet and operators lets us respond within hours, with no dependence on subcontractors, and ensures the intervention is carried out with the required rigour.

## Safety first

Every operation is preceded by site analysis, load assessment and a manoeuvring plan. Ground teams follow strict safety protocols, with marked-off areas and continuous communication between the crane operator and the procedure lead.
