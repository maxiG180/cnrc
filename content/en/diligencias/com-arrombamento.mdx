## Preparation and operational briefing

Procedures with forced entry require careful preparation. On the eve of every operation a **full briefing** is held, where the team reviews:

- The court decision authorising the action
- The profile of the location and the debtor
- The police forces and legal parties present
- The inventory of operational resources to be deployed
- The contingency plan for risk scenarios

## Execution on the ground

Each operation is carried out by teams trained in controlled-access techniques, with **fully equipped vehicles** and real-time documentation of every action. The goal is always to ensure that the forced entry is proportionate, legally impeccable and as non-invasive as possible given the assets involved.

## Post-procedure

After the intervention, we produce a detailed report with photographic record and inventory of assets, ready to be filed with the court. Documentary traceability is complete.
