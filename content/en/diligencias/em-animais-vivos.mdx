## Animal welfare as a priority

Procedures involving live animals — cattle, equines, dogs, poultry or other species — are conducted with full respect for animal welfare. We work with trusted veterinarians for prior assessment and follow-up during and after the seizure.

## Transport and holding

We have transport means adapted to different species and sizes, as well as in-house facilities for temporary holding. The final destination of the animals is always defined in coordination with the court and the competent authorities.

## Regulatory compliance

The entire operation runs in strict compliance with national and EU legislation on animal welfare, livestock transport and veterinary health.
