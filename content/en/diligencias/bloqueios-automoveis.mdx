## Rapid intervention, definitive result

Vehicle immobilisation is one of the most effective ways to secure an asset while awaiting removal. Our team fits the device within minutes, without resorting to invasive methods and fully respecting the debtor's property.

## Coordination with specialist tow trucks

Whenever the procedure requires subsequent transport, we coordinate the arrival of a tow truck appropriate to the type of vehicle or machinery. The operation runs in a coordinated sequence, minimising total intervention time.
