## Rigour and respect

Residential intervention is perhaps the most sensitive of all procedures. We act only with judicial authorisation and in the presence of the competent authorities, following protocols that simultaneously protect the rights of the creditor, the debtor and the household.

## Proportionality

Each procedure is scaled to the object of the action. Where possible we avoid forced entry, prioritising dialogue and voluntary cooperation. Only once these avenues are exhausted do we move to more incisive measures.

## Post-intervention

At the end, the property is properly secured and handed over to legal custody. A detailed report with photographic record is sent to the court and to the parties involved.
