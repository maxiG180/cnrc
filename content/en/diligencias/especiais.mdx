## When the operation demands more

Special procedures are those where case complexity — asset size, party profile, media sensitivity or logistical challenges — calls for planning beyond the routine. Each special procedure is treated as its own project, with a dedicated case manager.

## Resources deployed

These operations often combine the CNRC helicopter, multiple specialised vehicles, expert assessment teams and, where needed, cooperation with police forces or regulatory authorities. The mobile kitchen and private chef ensure complete autonomy during the operation.

## Discretion and safety

All planning takes place under strict confidentiality protocols. Execution is conducted with as much discretion as the scale of the operation allows, always preserving legality and the integrity of everyone involved.
