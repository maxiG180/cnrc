## A unique differentiator in the market

CNRC is the only credit recovery firm in Portugal with its own mobile kitchen and private chef. This investment lets us sustain the pace of operations that span several days, in locations with no access to support facilities.

## When it makes a difference

Procedures in rural areas, scattered farms, large multi-team operations and nocturnal interventions all benefit directly from this autonomy. Teams eat on site, with no unnecessary travel, preserving chain of custody and perimeter security.
