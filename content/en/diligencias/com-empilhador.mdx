## For industrial and logistics environments

In procedures involving the seizure of stocks, raw materials or heavy equipment, using our own forklifts dramatically accelerates the operation. Our operators work in close coordination with the team responsible for the inventory.

## Full traceability

Every pallet, box or piece of equipment is identified, photographed and logged in the inventory before being moved. The final report makes it possible to fully reconstruct the procedure, providing complete transparency for the court and the parties involved.
