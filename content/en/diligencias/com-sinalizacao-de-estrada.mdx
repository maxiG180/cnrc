## Intervention planning

Before any procedure on a public road, we draw up a temporary signaling plan covering the type of road, the estimated duration and the safety perimeter required. Where required, the plan is submitted to the competent authority for formal approval.

## Execution on the ground

The signaling team arrives before the operation begins, deploys the full scheme and only after validation does the procedure start. At the end, the road is restored to fully safe conditions for traffic.
