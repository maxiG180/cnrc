First-quarter results from the main banks operating in Portugal confirm a continued decline in non-performing loan (NPL) ratios. According to Bank of Portugal data, the downward trajectory remains sustained, despite inflationary pressure and a demanding macroeconomic context.

## The role of credit recovery firms

The work of firms specialised in extrajudicial and judicial credit recovery has been one of the pillars of this decline. The ability to act quickly on the ground and deploy distinctive operational resources is recognised as a decisive competitive advantage.

## Regulatory outlook

The European regulatory framework continues to push banks toward accelerated NPL reduction, with specific guidelines from the ECB and the EBA. The sector is expected to maintain its active policy of selling and recovering these portfolios throughout 2026.
