The High Council of the Judiciary has published its quarterly review of judicial court activity, highlighting a significant reduction in pending enforcement cases. This indicator is particularly relevant for creditors and for the entire credit recovery sector.

## Contributing factors

Factors cited for the decline include the growing digitalisation of procedural acts, faster action by enforcement agents and the involvement of credit recovery firms with in-house operational resources able to execute procedures in shorter timeframes.

## Impact on non-performing loans

The drop in pending cases has a direct effect on the profitability of overdue credit portfolios, allowing holders of these assets to recover capital in shorter cycles. This momentum is especially visible in real-estate portfolios and in enforcement against high-value movable assets.

## Next steps

The structural challenge remains: accelerating the judicial phase without compromising the rights of the parties. The procedural reforms planned for 2026 should further reinforce the trend of declining backlogs.
