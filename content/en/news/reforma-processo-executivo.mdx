The Council of Ministers has approved a bill to revise the Civil Procedure Code, with particular focus on the rules governing enforcement proceedings. The proposal now moves to parliamentary debate and could be approved in the first half of the year.

## Main changes

Key changes include:

- Full digitalisation of procedural acts, with mandatory qualified electronic signatures
- Shorter deadlines for decisions on forced entry and removals
- A strengthened role for credit recovery firms in the enforcement phase
- A new coordination platform connecting courts, enforcement agents and police authorities

## Implications for the sector

Credit recovery firms that already operate with advanced digital resources and their own operational capacity — such as CNRC — should benefit directly from the new framework. The reform implicitly recognises the importance of these operators to the effectiveness of the justice system.
