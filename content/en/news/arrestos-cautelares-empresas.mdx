Recent data published by the Directorate-General for Justice Policy show a significant rise in precautionary seizure requests filed in Portuguese courts over the past quarter. The indicator reflects creditors' efforts to secure asset coverage before debtors' financial positions deteriorate further.

## Hardest-hit sectors

Construction, non-food retail, freight transport and hospitality concentrate the largest number of measures. Speed in executing seizures is decisive: delays of just a few days can translate into a loss of asset value.

## The importance of rapid action

CNRC has shown that judicial orders can be obtained and precautionary seizures executed within very short timeframes — four days on average — thanks to advance case preparation and direct coordination with judges and enforcement agents. This capability is particularly relevant in scenarios where there is an imminent risk of asset dispersal.
