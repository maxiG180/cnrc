CNRC has announced the consolidation of its office in Huelva, Spain, in line with the strategy of expansion across the Schengen area initiated three years ago. The new unit will cover, with operational proximity, the entire southern border between Portugal and Spain, as well as western Andalusia.

## Cross-border cooperation

The presence in Huelva facilitates coordination with Spanish and Portuguese authorities in cases involving assets and debtors with links to both territories. CNRC maintains bilingual teams with in-depth knowledge of the judicial procedures of both countries.

## Six offices, one integrated capability

With Huelva consolidated, CNRC operates from six locations: Barreiro, Coimbra, Lisbon, Montijo, Vila Verde and Huelva. This network ensures broad territorial coverage and fast response anywhere in the Schengen area.
