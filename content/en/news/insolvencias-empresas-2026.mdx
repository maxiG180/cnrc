Quarterly statistics published by the DGSI point to a stabilisation in the number of corporate insolvencies in Portugal, closing the first quarter of 2026 in line with the same period a year earlier. The most exposed sectors continue to be non-food retail, hospitality and construction.

## Distribution by sector and region

The Lisbon and Tagus Valley region concentrates the largest absolute number of cases, but the North and Centre regions show the highest relative variation. Construction emerges as the sector with the highest growth in case volume.

## SME resilience

Small and medium-sized enterprises continue to show high resilience, supported by extrajudicial recovery plans and investment support programmes. Early intervention at the pre-insolvency stage has proved to be a critical factor in the survival of these companies.
