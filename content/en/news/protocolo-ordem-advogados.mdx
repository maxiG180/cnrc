CNRC — Camacho & Nunes Recuperação de Crédito has signed a cooperation protocol with the Portuguese Bar Association aimed at strengthening institutional coordination between the credit recovery sector and the legal profession. The document was signed this week in Lisbon.

## Scope of the protocol

The protocol defines:

- Formal communication channels for shared cases
- Joint training activities on the means and limits of extrajudicial recovery
- A code of best practice for coordination with legal counsel
- Streamlined procedures for responding to client complaints

## Ongoing training

As part of the protocol, CNRC commits to delivering regular training to registered lawyers on topics such as operational resources, preparation of precautionary seizures and judicial expertise. The first session will be held in April at the Bar Association headquarters in Lisbon.
