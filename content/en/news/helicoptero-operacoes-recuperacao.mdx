The use of in-house aerial resources is one of the most distinctive features of CNRC — Camacho & Nunes Recuperação de Crédito. For several years we have been the only firm in the sector in Portugal and across the Schengen area to operate an in-house helicopter on judicial procedures.

## When aerial support makes the difference

In operations to locate movable assets — vehicles, machinery or animals in particular — the aerial perspective dramatically shortens identification time and significantly increases the chance of seizure. In hard-to-reach areas the helicopter allows effective coordination with ground teams, avoiding premature interventions that would compromise the procedure.

## Integration with other resources

The aircraft does not operate in isolation: it forms part of a complete operation that includes special vehicles, forced-entry equipment, cranes and a mobile kitchen. This operational autonomy enables intervention anywhere in the country within very short timeframes.

## Next step

CNRC continues to invest in on-board technology, namely thermal imaging systems and encrypted communications, reinforcing the technologically advanced nature of this capability — unique in the sector.
