The Portuguese real estate market maintained moderate growth in the first quarter of 2026, with the median value of residential transactions rising. Lisbon, Porto and the Algarve continue to concentrate most of the dynamism, although regions such as the Alentejo coast and Madeira are showing significant growth.

## Foreign investment

Foreign investment maintains a relevant role in the premium segment, particularly in manor houses, country estates and luxury villas. Fiscal stability and quality of life remain decisive factors in choosing Portugal as an investment destination.

## Rental market

The rental segment recorded strong demand, with pressure on rents in major urban centres. New public policy measures, currently under parliamentary discussion, could introduce structural changes throughout the year.
