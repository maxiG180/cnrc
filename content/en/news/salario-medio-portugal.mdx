Statistics Portugal (INE) has released the latest data on average wages in Portugal, confirming a nominal rise compared with the same period a year earlier. Even so, economists warn that the inflation effect must be deducted to assess the real impact on Portuguese households' incomes.

## Nominal and real trends

The published figures point to an increase in gross income in both the private and public sectors. However, when adjusted for cumulative inflation, the real gain remains modest and varies widely across regions.

## Impact on credit and consumption

The financial sector has been monitoring this development closely, paying particular attention to the impact on debt-service ratios for housing and consumer loans. Rising living costs have placed pressure on households, driving an increase in requests to renegotiate contracts.

## Outlook for the second half

The coming months will be decisive in understanding whether the upward trajectory continues and whether inflation converges towards the ECB's target range. The labour market will remain the main indicator to watch.
