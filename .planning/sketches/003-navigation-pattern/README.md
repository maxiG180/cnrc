# Sketch 003: Navigation Pattern

## Design Question
How do users navigate the office journey? Building on the full-bleed overlay layout and minimal elegant typography, this explores different ways users can orient themselves and move through the scroll journey of 4 offices.

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/003-navigation-pattern/index.html
```

Or in Windows:
```
start .planning/sketches/003-navigation-pattern/index.html
```

## Variants

- **A: Sidebar Nav** — Sticky sidebar on the right with dots and office names. Shows all offices at once, click to jump. Labels appear on hover. Classic and functional.

- **B: Bottom Progress** — Fixed progress bar at bottom showing current office name, progress fill, and dots. Sleek, minimal UI that stays out of the way. Progress fill animates as you scroll.

- **C: Chapter Titles** — Scroll-triggered chapter indicators that appear in center as you transition between offices. Cinematic, immersive, minimal UI. Includes animated scroll hint.

## What to Look For

- **Orientation**: Which helps you understand where you are in the journey?
- **Control**: Which gives the right balance of guidance vs. user control?
- **Visual Cleanliness**: Which stays out of the way while being useful?
- **Brand Fit**: Which feels most appropriate for the prestigious, cinematic experience?
- **Usability**: Which would you actually use to navigate between offices?
