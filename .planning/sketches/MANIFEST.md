# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Bold and prestigious redesign of the offices page. Creates a cinematic scroll journey showcasing CNRC's national network through dramatic full-screen sections. Each office is presented with interior photography emphasizing operational capability and professionalism. The design conveys authority, scale, and physical presence across Portugal.

## Reference Points
- Luxury real estate showcase pages
- Corporate headquarters presentations
- High-end professional services firms
