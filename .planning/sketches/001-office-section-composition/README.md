# Sketch 001: Office Section Composition

## Design Question
How should each office section be composed? This sketch explores the fundamental layout structure for presenting each office in the scroll journey - the relationship between imagery, typography, and information hierarchy.

## Variants

- **A: Full-Bleed Overlay** — Dramatic full-screen image with text overlaid on a gradient. Bold, cinematic, emphasizes the photography. Text sits on left with dark gradient for readability.

- **B: Split-Screen 50/50** — Equal balance between image and content, alternating sides per office. Clean, organized, gives equal weight to text and visuals. More editorial feel.

- **C: Asymmetric Floating** — Large image with content in a floating card that overlaps the image below. Modern, dynamic, creates depth. Cards alternate sides for visual rhythm.

## What to Look For

- **Visual Impact**: Which layout makes the strongest first impression?
- **Content Readability**: Which approach makes the office information easiest to read and digest?
- **Scroll Experience**: Which feels best as you scroll through multiple offices?
- **Brand Alignment**: Which composition best conveys "bold & prestigious"?
- **Flexibility**: Which will work best with varying image quality and aspect ratios?
